1.0     DEFINITIONS


1.1     “Certificate of Compliance” refers to a certificate issued by the MCMC or registered
        certifying agency upon the certification of Communications Equipment.


1.2     “Communications Equipment” refers to any network facilities, customer equipment or
        radiocommunications equipment.

1.3     “Compliance Approval” refers to certification issued by the MCMC or registered
        certifying agency to a specific model of Communications Equipment, confirming that it
        complies with the standards.


1.4     “Consumer1” refers to a person who purchases products or services through online
        shops, e-commerce marketplaces or social media platforms for his or her personal use.

1.5     “Importer” refers to an organisation or individual that brings Communications Equipment
        into the country either for sale or for personal use, and is subject to regulatory
        requirements under the Communications and Multimedia Act 1998 and Communications
        and Multimedia (Technical Standards) Regulations 2000.

1.6     “Local Merchant” refers to an Online Merchant that operates inside of Malaysia.

1.7     “Online Merchant1” refers to an organisation or individual selling products or services
        to the Consumer through online shops, e-commerce marketplaces or social media
        platforms. Online Merchant includes Local Merchant and Overseas Merchant.


1.8     “Online Shopping Platform1” refers to a digital service provider, sometimes also called
        “intermediary”, offering the space for and facilitate the interactions between Online
        Merchant and the Consumer, often in wider digital ecosystems that span different
        services or sectors (e.g. finance and logistics).

1.9   “Overseas Merchant” refers to an Online Merchant that operates outside of Malaysia.

1.10 “Special Approval” refers to approval granted by MCMC or registered certifying agency
      to any Communications Equipment which is used exclusively by the applicant for the
      applicant’s sole purpose, research or demonstration purposes or within defined
      parameters, such as location, technical specifications, time period, type or class of users
      or conditions of usage.


1.11 “Standards” refers to the applicable technical standards or proficiency tests including
      processes and procedures, whether set out in the current standards, mandatory
      standards, technical codes or Malaysian standards and established for the purpose of
      ensuring proficiency levels of persons and the safety, non-interference levels,
      performance levels and interoperability of any Communications Equipment.


2.0   BACKGROUND


2.1   In today's rapidly evolving digital marketplace, online shopping platforms have become
      a primary channel for the sale of Communications Equipment. With the increasing
      volume of transactions conducted online, it is essential to ensure that the
      Communications Equipment offered through these platforms conforms to standards and
      meets the necessary legal requirements.


2.2   To address these needs, the Guidelines for the Online Shopping Platform, Online
      Merchant, and Consumer in Electronic Commerce for Communications Equipment
      (“Guidelines”) is intended to provide clear and comprehensive guidance for all
      stakeholders involved in the online sale of the Communications Equipment.


3.0   OBJECTIVE


3.1   The Guidelines is intended to provide guidance to Electronic Commerce (“e-
      commerce”) stakeholders, encompassing Online Shopping Platform, Online Merchant
      and Consumer, with the primary goal of strengthening the Consumer protection.


3.2   The Guidelines outlines the general responsibilities of Online Shopping Platform, Online
      Merchant and Consumer in ensuring the Communications Equipment sold online adhere
      to the certification and labelling requirements as stipulated in the Communications and

                                                                                    Page 4 of 25
      Multimedia Act 1998 (“CMA 1998”) and its subsidiary legislations.


3.3   By   establishing   the   Guidelines,    Malaysian   Communications      and   Multimedia
      Commission (“MCMC”) aims to prevent the supply of non-standard Communications
      Equipment and promote a trustworthy online shopping environment.


4.0   LEGISLATIVE FRAMEWORK


4.1   MCMC is responsible for regulating compliance of Communications Equipment for use
      and sale in the market in accordance with the Communications and Multimedia
      (Technical Standards) Regulations 2000 (“TSR 2000”).


4.2   Under TSR 2000, the Communications Equipment includes any network facilities,
      customer equipment, or radiocommunications equipment. The detailed scope of the
      Communications Equipment is outlined in Annex 1.


4.3   TSR 2000 requires all Communications Equipment to be certified in accordance with
      the specified standards, to ensure its safety, interoperability and the prevention of radio
      frequency interference.


4.4   Certified Communications Equipment shall also bear a certification label, a mandatory
      certification mark referred to as MCMC label, as illustrated in Figure 1, to indicate that
      the Communications Equipment complies with standards as required under the TSR
      2000.




                                              MCMC LOGO

4.5   The MCMC label can be in the form of physical label such as sticker, embossed or
      engraved or in the form of digital or e-label whereby the MCMC label is stored within the
      operating system of the Communications Equipment, provided that the Communications
      Equipment comes with integrated display.


                                                                                     Page 5 of 25
4.6   MCMC may register certifying agencies for the purpose of certifying compliance with the
      specified standards or technical codes. At present, the registered certifying agency for
      the certification of Communications Equipment under the CMA 1998 is SIRIM QAS
      International Sdn. Bhd. (“SIRIM”).


4.7   Regulation 18 of the TSR 2000 allows any person to make an application to MCMC or
      SIRIM for the certification of Communications Equipment. Certification may be issued
      as either compliance approval or a special approval. However, under SIRIM’s
      certification program, compliance approval is granted only to local companies registered
      in Malaysia to ensure regulatory compliance and facilitate enforcement actions in cases
      of non-compliance.


4.8   Compliance approval may be granted to a specific model of Communications Equipment
      when MCMC or SIRIM is satisfied that the Communications Equipment complies with
      the specified technical codes or standards. The certification process for compliance
      approval may involve document evaluation, laboratory or field tests or a combination of
      them. The compliance approval enables the sale and use of the Communications
      Equipment within Malaysia, with local companies typically handle the certification
      application to distribute the Communications Equipment to the Consumer.


4.9   Special approval is granted for any Communications Equipment intended for exclusive
      use by the applicant. Any Communications Equipment granted with special approval is
      not meant for sale as its use is confined within defined parameters which may include
      but not limited to location, technical specifications, time period, type or class of users or
      other conditions of usage as specified in the special approval, as provided under
      subregulation 19(3) of TSR 2000. The certification process for special approval may be
      exempted from laboratory or field tests and the certification shall be based solely on a
      paper evaluation.


4.10 Communications Equipment is listed under the Customs (Prohibition of Imports) Order
      (“Prohibition of Imports Order”), which requires the importation of such
      Communications Equipment to be accompanied by a certificate of approval issued by
      SIRIM. The equipment must conform to the relevant technical codes, Malaysian
      Standards or international Standards as deemed fit for importation and in accordance
      with the CMA 1998 and TSR 2000. As such, a certificate of approval is a mandatory
      requirement for importing Communications Equipment into Malaysia pursuant to the
                                                                                      Page 6 of 25
      Prohibition of Imports Order.


4.11 The certification and labelling requirements as specified under TSR 2000 are also
      applicable to the Communications Equipment sold through online shopping platforms:

      4.11.1   The Online Shopping Platform and Local Merchant are responsible for ensuring
               that all Communications Equipment sold through the online shopping platforms
               complies with the certification and labelling requirements stipulated in the CMA
               1998 and TSR 2000.


      4.11.2   The Consumer purchasing the Communications Equipment via Online
               Shopping Platform is responsible for ensuring the Communications Equipment
               is certified before making any purchase. The Consumer should aware that
               when purchasing the Communications Equipment directly from Overseas
               Merchant, they are considered as an Importer under the TSR 2000. As such,
               the Consumer is responsible to obtain the Special Approval and Clearance
               Letter (“SACL”) from SIRIM to certify the Communications Equipment and
               authorise its importation into Malaysia. The SACL serves as both the
               certification and certificate of approval for the Communications Equipment
               purchased from Overseas Merchant.


4.12 The use or sale of any non-certified Communications Equipment or any
      Communications Equipment that does not bear the MCMC label is an offence under
      TSR 2000. A person who commits such offence shall, on conviction, be liable to a fine
      not exceeding RM300,000.00 or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding three (3)
      years or to both.

5.1   The relevant provisions under TSR 2000 in respect of the certification and labelling of
      the Communications Equipment are as follows:


      5.1.1    Regulation 14: All Communications Equipment which are required to be
               certified under these Regulations shall be so certified.


      5.1.2    Subregulation 16 (1): No person shall use, offer for sale, sell or have in his

6.0   ONLINE SHOPPING PLATFORM


6.1   The Online Shopping Platform is generally responsible for protecting Consumer,
      ensuring Online Merchant sells lawful products or services to Consumer through its
      online shops or e-commerce marketplaces or social media platforms and complies with
      the national laws and regulations. Under regulation 16 of the TSR 2000, it is an offence
      for any person who uses, offers for sale, sells or has in his possession with a view to
      sell any Communications Equipment which is contrary to the standards, not certified as
                                                                             Page 10 of 25
       required by TSR 2000, or has been certified but subsequently altered or modified and
       no longer complies with the standards, or does not bear a certification mark or label.


6.2    Therefore, Online Shopping Platform must ensure that Communications Equipment
       listed on their platforms are certified in accordance with the standards specified by
       MCMC to avoid engaging in illegal activities, such as the sales of non-certified
       Communications Equipment and to fulfill its role in protecting the Consumer.


6.3    Within the e-commerce ecosystem, Online Shopping Platform is generally responsible
       to ensure compliance for both Local Merchant and Overseas Merchant as described

7.0   ONLINE MERCHANT


7.1   Online Merchant comprising both Local Merchant and Overseas Merchant is required to
      comply with all applicable Malaysian laws and regulations governing the sale of
      Communications Equipment, including strict adherence to the certification and labelling
      requirements. Local Merchant must ensure that Communications Equipment is certified
      and properly labelled in accordance with CMA 1998 and TSR 2000. Meanwhile,
      Overseas Merchant must transparently disclose the certification status of the
      Communications Equipment and provide guidance to Consumer on the steps needed to
      achieve compliance with the specified standards under CMA 1998 and TSR 2000. By
      fulfilling these responsibilities, both Local Merchant and Overseas Merchant contribute
      to the safety, reliability, and regulatory compliance of the Malaysian market.


Local Merchant


7.2   As importer of the Communications Equipment, Local Merchant is responsible to
      undertake the following actions:


      7.2.1    Ensure Communications Equipment offered on online shopping platform is
               certified and bears the MCMC label as an attestation of compliance with the
               relevant technical codes.


      7.2.2    Provide clear evidence of the certification status in the product description
               section on the online shopping platform. The evidence may include:


               (1)   MCMC Label: A visible mark on the Communications Equipment or its
                     packaging which indicates that the Communications Equipment is duly
                     certified and safe for the Consumer.


                                                                                  Page 13 of 25
              (2)   Copy of Certificate of Conformity (“CoC”): Upload or display a copy of
                    the official certificate i.e. the CoC issued by SIRIM that proves the
                    Communications Equipment has met the required standards under TSR
                    2000.


      7.2.3   Ensure that all details of the Communications Equipment provided are correct
              and accurate. This includes the model number, brand, and a clear description
              of the Communications Equipment. Providing precise information helps
              Consumer makes informed decision and ensures compliance with the laws and
              regulations.


      7.2.4   Cooperate with MCMC in providing information and documentation upon
              request, as and when required.


Overseas Merchant


7.3   Communications Equipment sold by Overseas Merchant is deemed as not certified for
      use in Malaysia. Accordingly, Overseas Merchant is responsible to:


      7.3.1   clearly state in the product description that the Communications Equipment is
              not certified for use in Malaysia. Consumer must be informed that uncertified
              Communications Equipment cannot be legally imported or used without proper
              certification and import permit.


      7.3.2   inform Consumer that they are responsible for obtaining the SACL from SIRIM.
              The SACL is necessary to certify, import and use of Communications
              Equipment in Malaysia.


      7.3.3   provide Consumer with all essential information required for the SACL
              application. This includes accurate details such as the model number, brand, a
              clear description, and a link to the Communications Equipment’s brochure or
              specification sheet.


      7.3.4   offer clear guidance to Consumer on the SACL application process. This
              includes explaining the steps, providing required documentation, and offering
              any necessary support to help Consumer meet compliance requirements.
                                                                               Page 14 of 25
8.0    CONSUMER


8.1    Consumer should engage in secure and responsible online shopping by purchasing only
       certified Communications Equipment. This ensures that the Communications Equipment
       that will be used by the Consumer complies with the standards required under the TSR
       2000, hence it is safe to use, interoperable, and does not cause radio frequency
       interference.


8.2    Before purchasing any Communications Equipment on an Online Shopping Platform,
       Consumer is encouraged to follow the following rules:


       Communications Equipment sold by Local Merchant


       8.2.1    Ensure Communications Equipment is not illegal as outlined in Annex 3;


       8.2.2    Look for regulatory information such as MCMC label or CoC of the
                Communications Equipment being sold;


       8.2.3    Verify the certification status of the Communications Equipment using the
                ‘Check Your Label’ (“CYL”) mobile application as per Annex 4 or through the
                SIRIM’s Online Certification for Communications and Multimedia Product
                (“eComM”) Website as per Annex 5;


       8.2.4    Purchase only certified Communications Equipment;


       8.2.5    Report non-certified Communications Equipment to MCMC; and


       8.2.6    Upon receiving the Communications Equipment, Consumer is encouraged to
                check for the MCMC label on the device, packaging, or warranty card to ensure
                that the equipment is certified and complies with applicable laws and
                regulations.


      Communications Equipment sold by Overseas Merchant

       8.2.7    Ensure Communications Equipment is not illegal as outlined in Annex 3;


                                                                                Page 15 of 25
      8.2.8    Look for technical specifications or brochures and ensure the product can
               comply with the local requirements (e.g. wireless devices shall operate within
               the frequency range and transmit power permitted for local use);


      8.2.9    Apply for the SACL through the SIRIM eComM website. The process flow for
               the SACL application is outlined in Annex 6;


      8.2.10   Upon receiving the SACL, the Consumer may proceed with the purchase of the
               Communications Equipment; and


      8.2.11   Alternatively, the Consumer can make the purchase and apply for SACL
               concurrently. However, the importation of the Communications Equipment is
               subject to the SACL approval.

                 ANNEX 6 - SPECIAL APPROVAL AND CLEARANCE LETTER


Step 1: Register online
Go to the SIRIM QAS eComM website and sign up as an 'Individual'.
Complete your profile details and submit. SIRIM QAS will email you an activation code.
Use the activation code to activate your account on the eComM website and complete
registration.




Step 2: Submit product clearance application
Click on 'Product Clearance Letter' and select 'New Product Clearance Application'.
Next, fill in the details of the seller/sender and the shipment.

Step 3: Prepare for clearance

Once payment is made, you will receive your SACL within 24 hours through the SIRIM eComM
website.

Download the SACL from the SIRIM eComM website and provide it to Customs or your
Forwarding Agent for customs clearance.
