What sort of certification mark or label must be used fo all certified communications equipment?
All communications equipment is required to display an MCMC label, either in 
digital
 or 
physical form
.
For equipment with an integrated display screen, the MCMC label may be 
embedded digitally 
within the operating system.
For 
physical label
, the MCMC label can be in form of sticker, embossed, engraved or printed onto the surface of an equipment.
