TikTok Shop by Tokopedia Prohibited Products Guidelines
04/05/2025
Applies to:Indonesia
Overview 
1.1 Introduction 
The
 TikTok Shop by Tokopedia Prohibited Products Guidelines
 have been drafted with the express purpose of:
Building a positive shopping environment on our platform; and
Providing a shopping experience that consumers can have confidence in.
All sellers who offer products for sale via TikTok Shop by Tokopedia must do so in accordance with these guidelines.
1.2 Scope 
These
 TikTok Shop by Tokopedia Prohibited Products Guidelines
 cover classes of products
that
sellers are prohibited from offering/importing when selling their products on TikTok Shop by Tokopedia.
All sellers must not list, sell, ship or promote any of the prohibited products set out in these guidelines. TikTok Users are also restricted from sharing or creating content involving the prohibited products set out in these guidelines.
Important Note:
 We may take enforcement actions against sellers or creators who breach these guidelines in accordance with our 
Seller Terms of Service
. 
Sellers are also responsible for ensuring that the listing, sale, and provision of products on TikTok Shop by Tokopedia comply at all times with applicable laws and regulations.
These guidelines are intended to facilitate seller compliance. They are not exhaustive and not intended as legal advice. We encourage sellers to seek independent legal advice if they have questions about the laws and regulations concerning their products.
In addition to these Prohibited Products Guidelines, the 
TikTok Shop by Tokopedia Restricted Product Guidelines
apply to products that require specific product category
approval. Sellers must adhere to both guidelines when selling on our platform. 
These Prohibited Products Guidelines are updated periodically. Sellers are responsible for checking this page regularly to ensure that they comply with our current guidelines.
 2. Definitions 
Sellers
 refer to any individual or corporation that sells items on TikTok Shop by Tokopedia. 
Content 
refers to all forms of media that can be uploaded by creators/sellers to TikTok Shop by Tokopedia. This includes all available formats such as e-commerce shop windows, videos, and live streams.
Prohibited Products 
refer to products that are either banned by local law/regulation or are not allowed for sale on TikTok Shop by Tokopedia.
Restricted Products 
refer to certain types of products that require prior approval from TikTok Shop by Tokopedia to be sold. Sellers who wish to sell restricted products may be required to pass a category approval process before selling such products. This process may require sellers to submit documents including but not limited to certifications, testing reports, product or package images, and other regulatory documentation.

Ammunition and Weapons

Deactivated firearms and weapons designed to fire blanks (such as starting pistols).
Toy guns or model guns that are not clearly listed as toys 
Toy guns that could be mistaken for authentic weapons (e.g., with similar colours, textures or materials that copy authentic guns) 
All associated parts, kits, ammunition, components and accessories for firearms. 
This includes but is not limited to:
Internal firearm accessories such as bullets, magazines, barrels, weapon conversion kits and firing pins
External firearm accessories such as silencers, gun stocks, bolts, fire control groups and trigger groups
Knives and other bladed products.

3.3 Human Body Parts and Remains
Human body parts and remains are prohibited from sale on our platform.
This includes but is not limited to:
Human body parts
Human waste or bodily fluids
Human remains
Sperm, egg cells, fertilized eggs, embryos, surrogacy, or intermediary services
 (buying and selling sperm, egg cells, fertilized eggs, embryos or surrogacy)
Any product that is made from or contains human body parts. For example, wigs comprising real human hair.

This includes but is not limited to: 
Live protected/endangered animals
Any protected/endangered animal body parts
Any animal or animal products made from protected/endangered animals
Non-exhaustive examples of products made from endangered/threatened animals include organs, horns, ivory, bone, skin, fur, wool, leather, or teeth
Non-exhaustive examples of animals protected by local laws or regulations include rhinos, orangutans and elephants
Aquatic resources that are banned from exploitation

Products that contain plants or other wildlife protected by local law and regulations
Aquatic products with natural toxins that are dangerous to human life
Livestock breeds that are not permitted by law for production and trading
Livestock breeds that are harmful to human health, the environment, the ecosystem, and livestock gene sources
Plant varieties that are not permitted by law for production and trading
Plant varieties that are harmful to production, human health, the environment and the ecosystem

Any product that appears to be designed for use in sexual/adult activity. This includes, but is not limited to: sex toys and games, erotic clothing, bondage gear, accessories, fetish wear, sexual enhancers, artificial hymens, or any other adult products depicting or suggestive of sex
Dating, sexual contact or escort services, including in-person or online services

Consumer goods, such as medicines, supplements, food/beverages, and beauty/personal care products, that have been repacked by the seller

We reserve the right to judge whether or not a listing is appropriate. If we determine that a product is prohibited, potentially illegal, or inappropriate, we may remove it from our platform.
Information and Audit 
We may review and audit product information at any time. Additional audits may be performed to identify if any change in listing information violates our guidelines above. Products that are found to violate our Product Listing Guidelines may be removed from the platform.
We may temporarily or permanently suspend or remove the selling privileges of sellers who commit severe or repeated violations. We reserve the right to report sellers who violate local laws and regulations to the necessary legal authorities where appropriate.
Appeal 
On occasion, our systems may block or remove product listings that are permitted to be sold on our platform. This usually occurs if incomplete or inaccurate product information is provided to us or if our systems incorrectly identify a product as prohibited. 
Sellers should be mindful that some products that are legally able to be sold in their country are still prohibited under TikTok Shop by Tokopedia policies. 
Sellers who believe their products were removed in error should raise a ticket through the Seller Center to review the restriction.
